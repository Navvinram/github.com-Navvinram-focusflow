ML-based personal productivity planner

FocusFlow

Tell it how you slept and how you feel. FocusFlow predicts your productivity and distraction risk for every hour and shows the best times to do your hardest work.

Open the live app View code on GitHub

The app may take a moment to wake up if nobody has used it recently.

What it does

Predicts productivity

A regression model gives a 0–100 score for each hour of the day.

Predicts distraction

A classifier estimates the chance you will be distracted at each hour.

Finds your best hours

Ranks hours by productivity and distraction risk to suggest when to focus.

Compares models

Linear, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting, scored on unseen test days. Gradient Boosting comes out best.

Watch the explainer

A 29-second animated walkthrough made from the app's own models: check-in, hourly forecast, and the actions that help most.

How to use it

  1. Open the live app.
  2. Set your sleep, phone usage, stress, energy and motivation in the sidebar.
  3. The plan updates as you move the sliders. Check your hourly forecast and best focus hours.
  4. Open Actions to improve for tips, and Regression comparison to see the best model.
